### Tam İngilizce seslendirme metni (60 kelime; sınır 69)
An embarrassing T-shirt. How many remember? Your guess is probably wrong. The wearers were asked: how many will remember? Average guess: forty-six percent. Reality: about twenty-three percent. Half. It's the spotlight effect: we feel centered on stage; others are in their own scenes. Later studies agree, but no large, preregistered replication exists. Subscribe, and we'll leave the light on.
