### Tam İngilizce seslendirme metni (67 kelime)
In space, fire has no up. Guess: on Earth, a flame is a teardrop. In orbit, what shape? On Earth, hot gas rises and stretches the flame. On the station, everything is in free fall. Gravity is there, but nothing rises. Oxygen mostly drifts in. The flame becomes a blue ball, with far less soot. Without up, even fire changes shape. Subscribe, and I'll quiz you again.

### Tam İngilizce seslendirme metni (70 kelime)
Fully cut the nerve: no water wrinkles. People say skin swells with water. Actually, nerves control it: blood vessels narrow. A 1930s observation: after a fresh, complete nerve cut, no wrinkling. One hypothesis: the wrinkles are like rain-tire treads, giving better grip on wet surfaces. One test found a wet-grip edge. Another found none. Why do they wrinkle? The jury's still out. Subscribe, and check your fingers next bath.
